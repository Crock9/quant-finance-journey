## Projects

### 1. Australian Equity Factor Lab
Planned research project investigating systematic factors in Australian equities, beginning with momentum.

Key goals:
- Collect and clean market data
- Construct quantitative signals
- Backtest systematically
- Include transaction costs and realistic assumptions
- Measure risk-adjusted performance
- Test robustness and attempt to falsify results

- Fundamental analysis

## Learning Principles

1. Understand the mathematics before relying on automation.
2. Use AI to accelerate research, not replace reasoning.
3. Avoid look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, and overfitting.
4. Record unsuccessful experiments as well as successful ones.
5. Prefer reproducible evidence over unsupported investment opinions.
